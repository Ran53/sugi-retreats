Our Story

A Green Haven on 3 Acres

Nestled amidst lush coconut trees, mango groves, and a vibrant mix of flowering and vegetable plants, SUGI Retreats is your perfect escape from the city. Spread across a serene 3-acre property, we offer a peaceful retreat where nature becomes your companion.

Whether you're looking for a private villa getaway, a cozy cottage with pool views, or budget-friendly dormitories for groups, we have something for everyone. Wake up to birdsong, take a dip in our high-pressure jacuzzi pool, and reconnect with the simple joys of rural Karnataka.

  • 3 acres of greenery
  • Coconut & mango trees
  • Flowering & vegetable gardens
  • Near Mysore, Karnataka
