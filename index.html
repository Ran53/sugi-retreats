Welcome to SUGI

A 3-Acre Botanical Sanctuary

Immerse yourself in nature at SUGI Retreats. Spread over 3 lush acres, our property is a thriving farm stay surrounded by dense coconut palms, sweet mango trees, and an array of flowering and vegetable plants.

Whether you choose our spacious 2 BHK villa with a unique balcony attic, one of our 4 charming cottages (pool or greenery facing), or our group-friendly dormitories, every corner offers absolute tranquility near Mysore.

  • 3 Acres of Organic Farmstead
  • Coconut & Mango Groves
  • Swimming Pool + Pressure Jacuzzi
